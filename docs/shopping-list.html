From the original speaker

  • 2 full-range drivers, about 52 mm, 4 Ω. Measure them before you print: measurements.md.
  • Passive radiator, if the unit has one. Only for bass_mode = "passive_radiator".

Tools

  • Soldering iron and solder; the iron also sets the heat-set inserts
  • Multimeter, wire strippers and flush cutters
  • Small Phillips screwdriver and a 2 mm hex key for the M3 screws
  • Hot glue gun
  • Craft knife, steel ruler and a fine file for the acrylic
  • 3D printer with at least 160 mm of Z height

Stores change prices and stock often, so treat every figure as a guide. The same list, with more notes, is in BOM.md. How it all goes together is in the build guide.
